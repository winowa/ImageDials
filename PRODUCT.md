# Product

## Users and purpose

ImageDials supports desktop and tablet users who turn image ideas into adjustable prompt controls, generate images, inspect provenance, and review saved results. Implementation guidance is in `.agents/skills/imagedials/SKILL.md`.

## Voice and constraints

Functional, concise, specific. Show data, controls, necessary instructions, errors and provenance. Avoid marketing prose, decorative headings, claims about design quality and explanations of obvious controls. Preserve the owner's dark default with a persistent light/dark control.

## Accessibility

Support desktop and 10–12-inch tablets in both orientations, readable controls, visible keyboard focus, accessible action names, and touch targets at least 44 CSS pixels. Emulation evidence must not be represented as a physical-device test.
